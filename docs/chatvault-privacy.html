Privacy Policy — ChatVault MD

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

ChatVault MD is designed as a 100% local-first Chrome extension. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or inspect your AI chat conversations.

1. Conversation Data

When you click the ChatVault MD icon, the extension reads the currently active tab exclusively inside your local browser memory to convert visible messages into Markdown, Obsidian, JSON, or printable HTML. Your chat text never leaves your device.

2. Local Storage

Saved Vault items and license activation state are stored locally in chrome.storage.local on your device and can be cleared anytime by removing the extension or clicking Deactivate.

3. Optional Pro License Verification

If you choose to purchase a Pro license key via Creem, only your license key and a random anonymous browser instance ID are transmitted to our license verification endpoint to confirm license validity. No browsing history, chat content, or personal information is transmitted.

4. Permissions Used

  • activeTab & scripting: Used solely when you click the extension icon to extract the current page's chat content locally.
  • storage: Used to store your optional Pro license state and local Chat Vault bookmarks on your device.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support, open an issue on the ChatVault MD support page.
